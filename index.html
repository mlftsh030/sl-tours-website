Cape Town Tours With a Local Team

SL Tours is a Cape Town-based tour operator offering guided day trips, Winelands wine tastings, Garden Route excursions, safaris, and airport transfers. Every tour is run by local guides who know the routes, the history, and the best spots to stop along the way. Whether you're visiting for a few days or exploring the Western Cape over a longer trip, we tailor each outing around your schedule and interests, using clean, well-maintained vehicles and drivers who show up on time.
